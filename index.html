I am currently a Lecturer, Master Supervisor, and University-appointed Associate Researcher at the College of Computer and Data Science, Fuzhou University. I was recognized as a Fujian Provincial High-Level Talent. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Artificial Intelligence, Xiamen University (XMU) under the supervision of Prof. Shaozi Li. I was also a joint Ph.D. student at the Department of Medical Imaging and Medical Biophysics, Western University (UWO), Canada, supervised by Prof. Shuo Li. I received my B.S. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2014. My research interests include intelligent medical image analysis, computer vision, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.
